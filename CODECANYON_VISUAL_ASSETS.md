## 2. Screenshot Module Details & Features Captured

1. **Authentication (`screenshot_01_login.jpg`):**
   - Clean split-screen layout with medical branding.
   - Dedicated 1-click demo role selector buttons (`Administrator`, `Doctor`, `Receptionist`).
   - Secure login form adhering to WCAG AA contrast standards.

2. **Executive Clinical Dashboard (`screenshot_02_dashboard.jpg`):**
   - High-density KPI cards: Total Patients, Today's Appointments, Inpatient Admissions, Monthly Revenue.
   - Quick action bar: *Add Patient*, *Schedule Appointment*, *Create Invoice*.
   - Recent appointments data table with live status badges.
   - Department bed occupancy bar chart.

3. **Patient Management (`screenshot_03_patients.jpg`):**
   - Search & filter toolbar (by department, admission status, blood group).
   - High-density tabular directory with Patient ID, Demographics, Contact, and Status.
   - Export actions: Excel/CSV and PDF summary.

4. **Appointment Scheduling (`screenshot_04_appointments.jpg`):**
   - Doctor and specialty dropdown filters.
   - Token-based daily appointment list.
   - Status indicators: Confirmed (Green), Pending (Amber), Completed (Blue).

5. **Billing & Invoicing (`screenshot_05_billing.jpg`):**
   - Real-time financial metrics (Total Billed, Total Paid, Outstanding Balance).
   - Printable A4 Hospital Invoice view with itemized consultation, lab, and pharmacy charges.
   - 5.00% Tax calculation and payment status stamps.

6. **Pharmacy & Inventory Management (`screenshot_06_pharmacy.jpg`):**
   - Stock level indicators with automated *Low Stock* and *Expiring Soon* alerts.
   - Batch tracking with expiry dates and unit pricing.
   - Medicine dispensation workflow.

7. **Laboratory & Diagnostic Tests (`screenshot_07_laboratory.jpg`):**
   - Test catalog management (CBC, Lipid Profile, Liver Panel).
   - Diagnostic orders tracking and biological reference range results drawer.
   - Formal printable laboratory report generation.

8. **Hospital Reports & Analytics (`screenshot_08_reports.jpg`):**
   - Monthly revenue trends area graph.
   - Inpatient admission vs discharge comparison.
   - Departmental patient volume distribution.

9. **System Settings & User Roles (`screenshot_09_settings.jpg`):**
   - Hospital profile configuration (Name, Address, Currency, Tax Rate).
   - User account management with Role-Based Access Control (Admin, Doctor, Receptionist, Pharmacist).

10. **Responsive Mobile & Tablet View (`screenshot_10_mobile.jpg`):**
    - Collapsible off-canvas mobile navigation drawer.
    - Touch-optimized card lists and compact action buttons.
    - Zero horizontal overflow.
